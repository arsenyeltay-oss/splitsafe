The product

See the danger day. Fix it. Check before you buy.

1 · The combined picture. Safe to spend until payday, the next short day and which payments cause it, and the debt-free date.
2 · How to fix it. Options re-simulated on the full calendar: move one payment to payday, pause a bill. The request message is written for you.
3 · “Can I afford this?” Before Pay in 4: SAFE, TIGHT or NO, the exact day it bites, the earliest safe date and a safer term.

How it works

Statement in, 90-day forecast out. Private by design.

1 · Import

Bank CSV or emails

The statement is parsed in the browser, never uploaded. Paycheck, bills and BNPL autopays (KLARNA*, AFFIRM*…) are found automatically. Or paste confirmation emails.

→
2 · Engine

Deterministic math

Paydays, schedules, a day-by-day simulation, fixes and affordability. Pure TypeScript, 91 unit tests, no AI in the math.

→
3 · Decide

Before money moves

Danger days, fixes, and “Can I afford this?” in the app and next to Pay in 4 at checkout (demo store + Chrome extension).

→
4 · Rescue

When income drops

What fails, what to pay first (rent → credit-reported → late fees), and a hardship request per provider. Always free.

Where AI is used, and where it isn’t

Claude only turns messy confirmation emails into JSON, validated with zod and confirmed by the user. Missing dates or amounts are flagged, never invented. Every number the user acts on comes from tested code.

No bank login, no server-side data

Data lives in the browser (localStorage). Only pasted email text is sent once for parsing, and it isn’t stored.

Stack: Next.js + TypeScript on Vercel; Anthropic Claude API (email parsing only); zod; Recharts; vitest. Screenshot: what the importer finds in a Chase-format CSV.SplitSafe · 5
